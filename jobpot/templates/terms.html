Terms of use

By using this Job Pot server you agree to the following.

Your accounts on other sites

You connect your own job site accounts and mailbox. You're responsible for following those sites' rules. Some sites, including LinkedIn, don't allow automated tools and may limit or close accounts that use them.

What gets sent in your name

Applications and email replies sent by Job Pot go out under your name, using the resume and details you provided. Check your profile, resume and criteria before turning on automatic applying.

No guarantees

Job Pot is provided as is. Job sites change without notice, so applications can fail, be sent incompletely, or not be sent. Fit scores are estimates, not advice. Keep your own copy of anything important.

Data

Your data stays on this server. The person who runs the server controls it, and is responsible for backups and for keeping it secure.

Acceptable use

Don't use Job Pot to send spam, to apply in someone else's name, or to collect other people's data.
