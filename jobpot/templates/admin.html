Sign-ups

When on, the sign-in page shows "Create an account" and anyone who can reach this Job Pot can make their own account. Leave it off if Job Pot is reachable from the internet and you only want people you add yourself.

Outgoing email

Used only to send "Forgot your password?" links. Until this is filled in, people who forget their password have to ask you to reset it above. Use an account made for this, for example an app password on a spare email address.

Local AI (Ollama)

When on, Job Pot uses your own Ollama model to explain fit scores, review resumes, sort employer emails and draft replies. Nothing leaves this server. When off or unreachable, it falls back to built-in rules.
