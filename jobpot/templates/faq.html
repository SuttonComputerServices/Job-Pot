Questions and answers

What does Job Pot do?

It searches the job sites you connect, scores every posting against your resume and your criteria, applies to the ones that clear your bar (where a site allows it), and reads the mailbox you give it so employer replies show up under the right application.

How is the fit score worked out?

Out of 100: up to 60 points for how many of your keywords appear in the posting, up to 30 for how much of the posting's vocabulary also appears in your resume, and 10 if the job title matches one of your wanted titles. A posting that contains one of your excluded words is skipped. If the administrator has turned on a local AI model, it can nudge the score and add a one-line reason.

Which sites can it apply on by itself?

LinkedIn Easy Apply, plus Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby job pages. SmartRecruiters jobs are found but you apply yourself. Each one has to be switched on separately on the Job sites page. If a form asks something Job Pot can't answer, or a site shows a captcha, the application is marked Needs you and the link is kept so you can finish it.

ZipRecruiter, Indeed and Monster block automated visitors, so Job Pot can't search or apply there. You can still add their jobs by hand and track their replies.

Do I need an account on Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby or SmartRecruiters?

No. They run companies' own careers pages, and anyone can apply on those without signing up. Job Pot only needs the company names. Type them, or paste a job link from that company and Job Pot takes the name out of it. For example, the Lever link https://jobs.lever.co/spotify/… gives spotify.

Why can't Job Pot use my.greenhouse.io?

MyGreenhouse is Greenhouse's own job search for candidates, and it needs you to sign in with a code emailed to you. Job Pot reads each company's public job board instead. You can still use MyGreenhouse to find companies: open a job there and paste its link into the Greenhouse box.

Could my LinkedIn account get restricted?

Yes, if you let Job Pot apply for you. LinkedIn's user agreement forbids bots and automated tools, and its help pages say accounts using them risk temporary or permanent restriction. Searching doesn't use your account at all: Job Pot reads LinkedIn's public job listings while signed out. The safest setup is to let Job Pot search LinkedIn, leave the sign-in empty and automatic applying off, and press Easy Apply yourself from the link on Applications. That's why automatic applying is off until you tick the acknowledgement for that site.

Which mailbox should I use?

A separate address used only for job hunting works best. Job Pot needs IMAP (to read) and SMTP (to send replies). Gmail and Outlook need an app password instead of your normal password.

Who can see my data?

Only you. Every record belongs to one account, and passwords for job sites and your mailbox are encrypted with a key that's unique to your account. The administrator can create and remove accounts but can't read your saved passwords in the app.

I lost my authenticator app.

Ask the administrator to turn off two-step sign-in for your account on the Administration page, then set it up again in Settings.

I forgot my password.

Use "Forgot your password?" on the sign-in page. If the administrator has set up outgoing email, a link valid for 30 minutes goes to the email address on your profile. If not, ask the administrator to set a temporary password for you.

How do I get an account?

Ask the administrator to add you, or, if they've allowed sign-ups, use "Create an account" on the sign-in page.

How often does it run?

Every 15 minutes by default, all day, as long as the worker container is running and automation is on in your Settings.
