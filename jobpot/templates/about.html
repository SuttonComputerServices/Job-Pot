About Job Pot

Job Pot is a self-hosted job search assistant. It runs on your own server, keeps everything in one database file there, and talks to the outside world only to fetch job postings, apply where you've allowed it, and check the mailbox you've set up.

Optional AI features use a model you host yourself through Ollama. No resume text or email is sent to a third-party AI service.

Version 0.1.0. Source code: github.com/SuttonComputerServices/Job-Pot.
